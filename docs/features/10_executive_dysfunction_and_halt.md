# Feature 10: Executive Dysfunction Support & Core Game Loop 🧠⚡

## 1. Overview & Research Basis
Individuals experiencing ADHD, autism, burnout, depression, or acute executive dysfunction struggle with initiation paralysis, task estimation, and persistent shame spirals. Drawing from clinical research on executive dysfunction and positive behavioral reinforcement, **And Friend** introduces supportive scaffolds designed to reduce cognitive friction to near zero.

---

## 2. Core Pillars

### A. Floating HALT Speech Bubbles
- **Dynamic Anchoring**: Floating reactive speech bubbles appear directly above the roaming pet avatar on the transparent overlay, tracking pet movement without obstructing active windows.
- **HALT Vulnerability Presets**: Targeted somatic and cognitive micro-check-ins addressing fundamental physiological triggers:
  - 🍎 **Hungry**: Prompting a sip of water or a quick nourishing snack (*"Water check whenever you're ready"*).
  - 😤 **Angry / Frustrated**: Prompting a 3-breath pause to break emotional flooding (*"Let's pause together for 3 deep breaths"*).
  - 🫂 **Lonely**: Gentle reminder to send a quick text or touch base (*"Sending a warm thought your way. You're not alone"*).
  - 🥱 **Tired**: Prompting an eye rest or offering to dig a burrow break (*"Taking things slow today is enough"*).
- **Grounded Copy Standard**: Strict prohibition against toxic positivity, coercive nagging, or scolding. Dialogue is non-demanding, validating, and compassionate.

---

### B. Micro-Task Chunking & Dopamine Feedback
- **1-Click Completion**:
  - Embedded inside HALT prompts and habit bubbles is an immediate, frictionless `"Done!"` action button.
  - Clicking `"Done!"` closes the prompt and triggers immediate multi-sensory celebratory dopamine feedback:
    - Confetti particle burst radiating from the companion.
    - Party hat or celebration accessory pop.
    - Victory dance animation with bouncy spring physics.
    - Special snack or collectible treat drop into the player's inventory.
- **Energy & Item Drops**:
  - Task completions refill companion energy meters and unlock room decorations, wearable stickers, and cosmetics.

---

### C. Forgiving Streak Protection System
- **3-Day Habit Rule**: Completing daily check-ins for 3 consecutive days automatically synthesizes and awards **1 "Streak Shield"** item (stackable up to 3 shields).
- **Automated Shield Consumption**: When life interrupts and a day is missed, an active Streak Shield is silently consumed to preserve the consecutive day streak count without breaking momentum.
- **Welcome-Back & Streak Repair**:
  - Returning after an extended hiatus greets the player with a warm welcome gift and a complimentary **"Streak Repair"** tool.
  - Allows players to retroactively mend broken streaks with a single click, abolishing the "what's the point of continuing" despair common in traditional habit trackers.
- **Burrow Freeze**: Entering the burrow mound automatically suspends streak decay entirely.
