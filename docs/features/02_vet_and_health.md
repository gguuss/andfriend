# Feature Spec: Dr. Paws Vet Diagnostic Clinic 🩺

## Overview
An interactive clinic where you can take your friend for wellness examinations, health diagnostics, and treatment when they are unwell, fatigued, or underfed.

## Gameplay Flow
1. **Triggering a Vet Visit**:
   - Accessed directly from the right-click menu or when happiness/hunger drops below critical thresholds (< 15%).
2. **The Examination**:
   - Interactive stethoscope tool: click and drag stethoscope over friend's chest to hear rhythmic heartbeats and purrs.
   - Temperature thermometer animation.
3. **The Diagnosis Card**:
   - Vitals overview (Hunger, Energy, Happiness, Stress).
   - Clinical commentary:
     - *"Patient is showing symptoms of extreme dumpling deficiency."*
     - *"Heart rate is elevated from doing 12 backflips in a row. Needs rest."*
4. **Prescription & Treatment**:
   - Administer Vitamin Treats or soothing bandages.
   - Restores health to 100% and awards the "Doctor's Good Patient" badge!
