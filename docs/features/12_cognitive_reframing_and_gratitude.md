# Feature 12: Cognitive Reframing & Success Mindset 💎✨

## 1. Overview & Research Basis
Negative Automatic Thoughts (NATs) and Negative Mental Attitudes (NMA) frequently trigger paralysis, imposter feelings, and chronic self-criticism during solitary computer work. Grounded in Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) and neuroplasticity studies, this module equips the companion to act as a supportive cognitive mirror, catching self-defeating loops and replacing them with grounded, present-tense reframing.

---

## 2. Core Pillars

### A. Positive Mental Attitude (PMA) vs. NMA Affirmation Cards
- **Cognitive Catching & Reframing**:
  - Interactive affirmation deck that identifies common NMA thought traps (all-or-nothing thinking, catastrophizing, personalization) and gently pivots to empowering PMA statements.
  - Examples:
    - *NMA Loop*: "I am so behind and wasting time." ➔ *PMA Reframe*: "Taking a measured breath helps me work clearer. Doing one small step right now is enough."
    - *NMA Loop*: "I can't get anything done today." ➔ *PMA Reframe*: "I choose to value myself today regardless of productivity metrics."
- **Present-Tense Daily Affirmation Deck**:
  - Tap the companion or affirmation deck to draw an uplifting, beautifully illustrated card with subtle shimmer shaders.
  - Clean, grounded prose with zero patronizing platitudes.

---

### B. Local Micro-Gratitude Logging
- **1-Sentence Encrypted Journaling**:
  - Optional, ultra-light micro-prompts triggered following task completions, mindfulness breaks, or emerging from the burrow.
  - Prompts: *"What is one small thing that felt good or gave you comfort today?"*
  - Requires only a single sentence or phrase, keeping friction non-existent.
- **SQLCipher AES-256 Local Encryption**:
  - All journal entries, gratitude notes, and personal thoughts are encrypted on disk via **SQLCipher with AES-256 GCM**.
  - **Zero Cloud Leak**: Complete privacy—data never leaves the local machine, requires zero external accounts, and is completely immune to third-party data mining.
