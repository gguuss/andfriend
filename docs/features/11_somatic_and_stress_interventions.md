# Feature 11: Somatic & Stress Interventions 🫁🌿

## 1. Overview & Research Basis
When cognitive exhaustion or acute sensory overstimulation takes hold, top-down cognitive reasoning often fails. Somatic interventions bypass executive cognitive fatigue by directly stimulating the parasympathetic nervous system via the vagus nerve, sensory grounding, and acupressure pathways.

---

## 2. Core Interventions

### A. Somatic EFT (Emotional Freedom Technique) "Tap with Friend"
- **Interactive Tapping Minigame**:
  - The companion leads an interactive co-regulation routine demonstrating gentle rhythmic tapping along key somatic meridian endpoints:
    1. **Eyebrow Point**: Between eyebrows to release mental tension.
    2. **Under Eye Point**: Cheekbone region to soothe anxiety and fear responses.
    3. **Chest / Sore Spot (K27 / Thymus)**: Below clavicle to stimulate vitality and chest relaxation.
    4. **Karate Chop Point**: Outer edge of the hand to reset emotional resistance and psychological reversal.
  - Accompanied by rhythmic soothing audio clicks/chimes and mirroring visual cues on the pet sprite.

---

### B. Guided Diaphragmatic Breathing (4-7-8 Rhythm)
- **Visual Breathing Bubbles**:
  - Paced visual expansion and contraction orbs anchored directly above or around the pet sprite on the desktop overlay.
  - Calibrated to the clinically validated **4-7-8 breathing cadence**:
    - **4 seconds**: Inhale through nose (expanding orb, companion inhales).
    - **1-7 seconds**: Gentle hold (orb pulses softly, companion holds breath).
    - **5-8 seconds**: Prolonged exhale through mouth (orb contracts gently, soothing chimes ring).
  - Stimulates the vagal brake, slows heart rate variability (HRV), and down-regulates sympathetic fight-or-flight arousal during acute panic or hyperventilation.

---

### C. 5-Sense Environmental Grounding
- **1-Minute Sensory Scans**:
  - Quick, guided micro-prompts to immediately interrupt racing thoughts and catastrophic rumination:
    - 👁️ **5 Things You See**: Notice shapes, light reflections, or colors in your room.
    - ✋ **4 Things You Can Feel**: Notice feet on the floor, texture of your desk or clothing.
    - 👂 **3 Things You Hear**: Notice ambient sounds, keyboard clicks, distant cars, or humming fans.
    - 👃 **2 Things You Smell**: Notice coffee, fresh air, or hand lotion.
    - 👅 **1 Thing You Taste**: Take a sip of water or notice the lingering taste of mint or tea.
  - One-click progression with gentle completion particles and immediate companion gratitude.
