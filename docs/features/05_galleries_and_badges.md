# Feature Spec: Friend Gallery, Badges & Unlocks 🏆

## Overview
A visual trophy gallery celebrating your friendship milestones, rewarding activities with collectable badges and wearable cosmetic items.

## Badges & Unlocks
| Badge | Requirement | Reward Cosmetic |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **First Steps** | Adopt your first friend | Star Ribbon |
| **Grand Trickster** | Master all 6 tricks | Magician Cape & Wand |
| **Master Sniffer** | Sniff 50 desktop files | Detective Fedora |
| **Burrow Architect** | Dig 15 corner burrows | Miner Helmet with Flashlight |
| **Night Owl** | Study together between 12 AM - 4 AM | Glowing Moon Pendant |
| **Best Friends** | Reach 100 Affection | Golden Friendship Crown |

## Visual Gallery
- Accessible from the right-click menu or top ribbon.
- Displays all owned companions and unlocked badges with shiny 3D badge rendering.
