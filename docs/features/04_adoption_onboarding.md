# Feature Spec: Adoption Center Onboarding 🏡

## Overview
A guided storybook onboarding experience when a user launches "And Friend" for the first time or chooses to adopt an additional companion.

## User Journey
1. **Welcome to the Sanctuary**:
   - Storybook illustrated modal: *"Every desktop deserves a faithful companion."*
2. **The Compatibility Questionnaire**:
   - 3 fun questions:
     - *"How do you spend your days?"* (Coding / Creating Art / Writing / Gaming)
     - *"What energy level do you prefer?"* (Quiet observer / Playful acrobat / Snoozy nap buddy)
     - *"What element calls to you?"* (Flame Fox, Forest Bunny, Cloud Dragon, Ocean Slime, Golden Shiba, Night Cat)
3. **The Reveal & Adoption Certificate**:
   - Presents 3 recommended friends based on the quiz.
   - Choose your buddy, enter its name, and sign an interactive adoption certificate!
   - Confetti burst and your new friend jumps right out onto your desktop!
