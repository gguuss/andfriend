# Feature Spec: Dynamic Personality Evolution 🧠

## Overview
Your companion's internal traits and temperament shift dynamically over time based on what activities and behaviors you encourage.

## Personality Dimensions
- **Studious vs. Whimsical**: Encouraged by sniffing code/document files vs. random wandering.
- **Athletic vs. Cozy**: Encouraged by frequent trick training vs. long naps in the corner burrow.
- **Affectionate vs. Independent**: Encouraged by frequent petting/tickling vs. leaving the friend to roam freely.
- **Foodie**: Encouraged by discovering exotic snacks and desktop cookies.

## Impact on Behavior
- **Studious**: Sits quietly beside your active editor window, wears glasses naturally, perks up when code is compiled.
- **Athletic**: Offers spontaneous backflips, jogs along the bottom dock, high-fives your cursor.
- **Cozy**: Spends more time snoozing in the burrow, slower breathing, gentle smiling eyelids.
