# And Friend - Product Roadmap 🐾

Welcome to the **And Friend** product roadmap. This document outlines the long-term vision, feature specifications, and phased milestones for creating the ultimate cross-platform desktop companion.

---

## 🧭 Phasing Strategy

Features are prioritized based on **local development feasibility** and **immediate user delight**:
1. **Phase 1: Foundation & Desktop Overlay** *(Completed)*
2. **Phase 2: Local Life, Growth & Care** *(Local-First, High Impact)*
3. **Phase 3: Adoption Onboarding & Badge Galleries** *(Progression & Customization)*
4. **Phase 4: Online Gathering Spaces & Viral Growth** *(Multiplayer & Social)*
5. **Phase 5: Mobile Companion App** *(Cross-Platform Cloud Sync)*

---

## 🗺️ Detailed Roadmap Milestones

### Phase 1: Foundation & Invisible Overlay *(Shipped)*
- [x] Fullscreen borderless transparent overlay with mouse pass-through (`setIgnoreMouseEvents`).
- [x] Free desktop roaming and draggable relocation.
- [x] Right-click contextual interaction ribbon on your companion.
- [x] Tomodachi care: Feeding, Sleeping, Tickling, Vitals (Hunger, Energy, Happiness, Affection).
- [x] Real-time eye gaze tracking to mouse cursor.
- [x] Desktop file & folder interaction (`~/Desktop` scanning, sniffing files, folder diving).
- [x] Corner burrow digging and den peeking.
- [x] Procedural chiptune sound synthesizer with instant mute toggle.
- [x] 6 Trick training playbook with XP mastery tiers.
- [x] Companion Builder Wizard with prompt generation and palette studio.
- [x] Rebranded to **"And Friend"** with **"Liil Buddy"** as the starter friend.

### Phase 2: Local Life, Growth & Care *(Next Up)*
*Focus: Deepening the emotional bond through local lifecycle mechanics without requiring servers.*

#### 🥚 1. Lifecycle: Egg, Hatching & Evolutionary Stages
- **Egg Stage**: Your friend begins as a gently rocking, speckled egg. Needs warmth, rhythmic clicking, and care to hatch.
- **Baby Stage**: Needs frequent feeding, burping, and gentle nap times. Slower movement, baby chirps.
- **Teen Stage**: Curious, energetic, learns tricks quickly, rebellious quirks (occasionally ignores commands if grumpy).
- **Adult Stage**: Fully evolved majestic appearance with specialized body features, accessories, and autonomous habits.

#### 🎂 2. Notion of Age & Deep Inspection
- Real calendar age tracking (Days with you, Birth date, Total naps, Total treats eaten).
- **Inspect Mode**: Zoomed-in inspection card showing favorite memories, temperament, trick mastery breakdown, and current physical condition.

#### 🩺 3. Dr. Paws: The "Vet" Diagnostic Clinic
- Interactive clinic visit when your friend feels sick, exhausted, or underfed.
- Stethoscope animation to listen to heartbeat / purrs.
- Diagnostic report: clinical breakdown of vitals, stress level, and prescriptions (e.g. *"Prescribed 2 warm dumplings and 10 minutes of sleep"*).
- Vitamin treats and wellness bandages.

#### 🧠 4. Dynamic Personality Evolution
- Internal characteristics that shift dynamically over time based on how you interact:
  - *Studious*: Boosted by frequent code/text file sniffing and trick practice.
  - *Playful / Hyper*: Boosted by daily tickles, races, and backflips.
  - *Cozy / Snuggly*: Boosted by long naps in the corner burrow and gentle head pats.
  - *Foodie*: Boosted by sampling diverse snacks and discovering new file treats.
- Personality determines autonomous dialogue, gait speed, sound pitch, and idle habits.

#### 🧘 5. Mindfulness & Owner Caretaker Module
- **Reciprocal Care**: The friend doesn't just receive care—they look after *you*.
- **Periodic Wellness Nudges**:
  - 💧 Hydration reminders (*"Take a sip of water, friend!"* with water droplets).
  - 🪟 Fresh air & eye rest prompts (*"Look out the window for 20 seconds!"*).
  - 🧘 Posture & shoulder drop check-ins (*"Unclench your jaw and drop your shoulders"*).
  - 💖 Affection & affirmation drops (*"You are doing great and you are loved!"* with heart bursts).
  - 🌙 Late-night wind-down whispers after midnight.
- **Interactive Sanctuary Modal**:
  - 4-4-4-4 Box breathing exercise synchronized with the friend's breathing animation and Tibetan singing bowl chimes.
  - Daily Hydration tracker button with celebration particles.
  - 1-minute calming eye-rest mode.

#### 📋 6. Daily Routine & Habit Tracker ("Eat Your Vitamins")
- **Shared Daily Habits**: The companion partners with the owner to maintain daily health and wellness routines.
- **"Eat Your Vitamins" (💊)**: Prominently featured core habit celebrating daily supplements & medicine.
- **Daily Checklist**:
  - 💊 Eat Daily Vitamins
  - 💧 Morning Hydration Boost
  - 🧘 Posture & Spine Reset
  - 🥗 Nourishing Meal Break
  - 🪟 20-20-20 Eye Rest
  - 🌙 Evening Wind-Down & Gratitude
- **Streak System**: Daily rollover preserves consecutive streak flames (`🔥 N-Day Streak`).
- **Companion Rewards**: Checking off habits grants +25 companion XP, raises affection, spawns celebration particles, and triggers praise bubbles.
- **Custom Habits**: Add and track personal productivity or health goals.
- **Autonomous Check-ins**: Companion gently nudges user if core routines remain untouched during the day.

### Phase 3: Adoption Onboarding & Badge Galleries
*Focus: The first-time adoption experience and rewarding long-term companionship.*

#### 🏡 5. Adoption Center Onboarding
- Guided storybook onboarding when first launching the app.
- Quiz / preferences: *"What kind of friend fits your workspace?"* (Quiet studious companion, energetic trickster, cozy desktop sleeper).
- Selecting, naming, and signing the official **Adoption Certificate**.

#### 🏆 6. Friend Badge Gallery & Activity Unlocks
- Trophy room displaying earned achievement badges:
  - *"Night Owl"* (Working past midnight together)
  - *"Clean Desk"* (Sniffing 50 desktop files)
  - *"Grandmaster"* (Mastering all 6 tricks)
  - *"Burrow Architect"* (Digging 10 dens)
- Badges unlock exclusive wearable cosmetics:
  - Graduation cap, detective magnifying glass, golden crown, holiday scarves, and tiny cloaks.

---

### Phase 4: Online Gathering Spaces & Viral Growth
*Focus: Connecting friends across the web with lightweight real-time hubs and social sharing.*

#### 🏖️ 7. Online Hangouts: The Zoo, The Café, The Beach
- WebRTC / WebSocket lightweight multiplayer rooms.
- Take your friend to:
  - **The Café**: Cozy lo-fi atmosphere where friends sip tea and study together.
  - **The Beach**: Sunny sandbars where friends splash in waves and dig sandcastles.
  - **The Zoo**: Meet exotic species and compare rare friend evolutions.
- Emote bubble chatting and friend-to-friend interactions (high-fiving another user's companion).

#### 🎮 8. Mini-Games with Friends
- **Park Catch / Fetch**: Throw a desktop frisbee or tennis ball for your friend to fetch.
- **Race Track**: Friendly races across the screen.
- **Rhythm Dance-Off**: Tap along to beats with other friends in the Café.

#### 📱 9. Social Media & Viral Growth
- **Snap & Share Card**: Generate animated GIFs and aesthetic status snapshots with one click.
- Direct export formatting for:
  - **Instagram Stories & Posts** (Square / 9:16 aesthetic friend polaroids)
  - **Threads** (Achievement cards with thought quotes)
  - **Facebook** (Milestone celebration cards)

---

### Phase 5: Mobile Companion App
*Focus: Never leave your friend behind.*

#### 📲 10. And Friend Mobile (iOS & Android)
- Built with shared Flutter codebase (`lib/models`, `lib/controllers`, `lib/graphics`).
- Pocket widget for iOS Lock Screen & Android Home Screen.
- Step-counter integration: walking in the real world gives your friend energy and unlocks special outdoor treats.
- Cloud sync (Firebase / Supabase) to keep your friend's age, vitals, and accessories synchronized between desktop and phone.
